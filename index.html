    🔍 How the Benchmark Fits In

    Most document parsing decisions are made on vibes. Someone tries a tool, it looks fine on a few clean PDFs, and it becomes the default. That breaks down the moment a real-world archive shows up with skewed scans, dense tables, and multi-column layouts.

    The puffinparse benchmark exists to replace that guesswork. It is open and inspectable, which means you can see the documents used, the scoring approach, and the raw results behind every number. When two providers disagree, you can look at the underlying evidence rather than picking whichever landing page looked nicer.

    🧭 Getting the Most Out of It

    1. Start with a sample. Take five to ten documents that represent what you actually work with — not the cleanest file you own.
    2. Run them through more than one provider. Comparing two or three parsers on your own material tells you more than any general ranking.
    3. Check the extracted text by hand. Confirm tables stayed tables and headings stayed headings, not just that letters were recognised.
    4. Use the benchmark to narrow the field. Once you have a shortlist, the inspectable results help you make the final call.
    5. Keep a consistent workflow. The single interface is the point — switching providers should not require rebuilding your process.
